   All 3 training scripts + checkpoint use identical:

2. LEAKAGE AUDIT (Issue B) — RESOLVED
   Near-duplicate detection (pHash Hamming ≤ 5/64, same-class):
   - Total pairs: 2 out of 407 images (0.5%)
   - Distance: both at dist=0 (byte-identical pHash)
   - Images with ≥1 near-duplicate: 4 (1.0%)
   - Session-clusters: 405 (403 singletons + 2 pairs)
   - Pairs crossing standard 5-fold boundaries: 1 (loh(54) ↔ loh(84))

   Standard stratified 5-fold CV:    95.08% ± 1.75%
   Leakage-free grouped 5-fold CV:   95.56% ± 1.72%
   Difference:                      +0.48 pp (grouped is HIGHER)

   Conclusion: The 95% CV is NOT inflated by leakage. Grouped CV
   confirms the same accuracy after removing all near-duplicate
   information from training folds.

  - Kaggle test:      84.90%

  - Transfer gap:    +50.75 pp (FAIL — public data does not transfer)

CONCLUSION:
Ethiopian field data produces Ethiopian field performance.
Public wheat datasets do not transfer to Ethiopian conditions.
407 images achieve 95% — the barrier is data collection, not ML
capability. Scaling to more sites and seasons (Phase C) will
produce a genuinely generalizable model.
