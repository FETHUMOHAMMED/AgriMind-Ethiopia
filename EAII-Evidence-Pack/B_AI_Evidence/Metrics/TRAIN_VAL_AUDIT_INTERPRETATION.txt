IMPACT ON VALIDATION METRICS AND CHECKPOINT SELECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
One byte-identical image was identified across the training and validation
partitions. Its effect on validation metrics and checkpoint selection has not
been independently quantified. A single changed validation prediction could
in principle change an epoch-to-epoch comparison; the 1/3,090 = 0.03pp
proportion of the validation set is a direct-count bound on the fraction of
affected examples, not a proof that epoch selection was unaffected.

A grouped, duplicate-controlled split is recommended for future model
selection to eliminate this class of ambiguity entirely.

IMPACT ON THE REPORTED TEST METRIC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The reported test metric (95.27% top-1 on 3,106 leakage-controlled images)
is unaffected by train/validation leakage. Test-set integrity was
independently verified via the test-side pHash + SHA-256 audit, which
identified 3 byte-identical duplicates; those are documented in the
Scenario A and Scenario B split variants and in CLASSIFICATION_REPORT_v2_TEST_*.txt.

RELATIONSHIP TO SCENARIO A/B
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Scenarios A and B are test-set variants. The train/validation boundary is
identical in splits.json, splits_scenarioA.json, and splits_scenarioB.json;
the finding in this audit therefore applies equally to all three.

PHASE 2 RECOMMENDATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All 4 confirmed byte-identical duplicates identified across the entire
audit (3 test-side + 1 val-side) belong to the same PlantVillage
"GHLB_PS ... Day 13" photography session. This is a structural artifact
of filename-based random splitting: images from a single photography
session scatter across partitions.

Fix for Phase 2: group images by photography session BEFORE random
partitioning, not by individual filename.
