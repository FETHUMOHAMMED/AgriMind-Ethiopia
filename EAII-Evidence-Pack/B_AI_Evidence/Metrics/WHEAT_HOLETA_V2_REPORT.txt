AgriMind Wheat Holeta v2 — Production Training Report
============================================================

Model: MobileNetV2, 3-class (healthy, septoria, stripe_rust)
Training data: 407 Ethiopian field images from Holeta wheat farm
Provenance: Kaggle olyadgetch/wheat-leaf-dataset (mirrors Mendeley Holeta)
Camera: Canon EOS 5D Mark III
Labels: Expert-verified with plant pathologists

Train/val split: 345 / 62
Best val_acc: 95.16% at epoch 2

5-FOLD CV EVIDENCE (from diagnostic run):
  Mean accuracy: 95.08% (±1.75%)
  Fold accuracies: 97.56, 95.12, 93.83, 92.59, 96.30

TRANSFER CONTEXT:
  Same 3 classes trained on 1,510 public Kaggle images:
    - Kaggle test: 84.90%
    - Holeta field: 34.15% (FAIL)

  Trained on Holeta field images:
    - Holeta field (5-fold CV): 95.08% (PASS)

CONCLUSION:
  Ethiopian field data is essential for Ethiopian field performance.
  407 images achieve 95% — the barrier is data collection, not ML capability.

DEPLOYMENT LIMITATIONS:
  - Single location (Holeta, Oromia)
  - Likely single growing season
  - 3 classes only (FHB, leaf_rust not covered)
  - Not tested at other Ethiopian sites
  - Treatment recommendations require agronomist review
