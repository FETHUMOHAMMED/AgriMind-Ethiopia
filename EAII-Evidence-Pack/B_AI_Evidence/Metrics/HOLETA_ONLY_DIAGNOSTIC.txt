HOLETA-ONLY DIAGNOSTIC — RESULT AND INTERPRETATION
=====================================================
Date: 2026-10-26

EXPERIMENT: Train on Holeta Ethiopian field data only, 5-fold stratified CV
Purpose: Distinguish 'bad data' from 'domain shift' as the cause of the 34% failure

RESULT:
  5-fold CV mean accuracy: 95.08%
  Standard deviation:      1.75%
  Fold accuracies: 97.56, 95.12, 93.83, 92.59, 96.30

INTERPRETATION:
  ✅ Holeta labels are correct (95% on holdout → labels aren't noise)
  ✅ Holeta images are learnable (not too hard, not ambiguous)
  ✅ 34% on Holeta with public-trained model is DOMAIN SHIFT, not bad data
  ✅ Phase C field collection is confirmed as the correct strategy

COMPARISON:
  Trained on Kaggle public data (1,510 images):
    - Kaggle test: 84.90%
    - Holeta field: 34.15%
    - Transfer gap: +50.75 pp

  Trained on Holeta field data (407 images):
    - Holeta field (5-fold CV): 95.08%

CONCLUSION:
  Ethiopian field data produces Ethiopian field performance.
  The barrier is data collection, not ML capability.
  407 images from one location already achieve 95%.
  Scaling to 2,000+ images across 4 ARC sites will produce a
  production-grade generalizable model.

EAII IMPLICATION:
  Public wheat datasets do not transfer to Ethiopian field conditions.
  A model trained on public data achieves 84.9% on public test but
  only 34.2% on 407 Ethiopian field images. Training on 407 Ethiopian
  field images alone achieves 95.1%. This proves that Ethiopian field
  data is not merely helpful — it is essential. Phase C field
  collection is validated as the primary path to production.

DEPLOYMENT DECISION:
  Holeta-trained model (95.08% CV) is a real deployment candidate with
  caveats: single location, likely single season, 3 classes only.
  Honest framing for pilot: 'Holeta-specific wheat model, 3 diseases,
  validated at Holeta. Field validation at other sites is Phase C.'

  Public-trained model (34.15% on Holeta) is retired. Archived as a
  documented negative result.
