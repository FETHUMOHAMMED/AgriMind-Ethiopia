WHEAT PHASE B — RESULTS AND LIMITATIONS
==========================================
Date: 2026-10-26

RESULTS:
  Public-trained wheat classifier:
    - Kaggle test set: 84.90% accuracy
    - Holeta Ethiopian dataset (407 images): 34.15% accuracy
    - Difference: 50.75 percentage points

  Holeta-only experiments:
    - 5-fold stratified CV: 95.08% ± 1.75% mean accuracy
    - Final production training (345/62 random split): 95.16% val accuracy

LIMITATIONS AND CAVEATS:
  1. Class-index consistency, preprocessing, and dataset integrity must be
     verified before attributing the full 50.75 pp gap to domain shift.
  2. The 95.08% CV result depends on split integrity. Near-duplicate images
     from the same plant, leaf, or session may cross fold boundaries and
     inflate the reported accuracy. A leakage-resistant grouped CV is
     required to establish the true generalization within the Holeta data.
  3. The 95.16% final-training validation is on a 62-image split from the
     same 407-image collection. It does not establish generalization
     beyond this collection.
  4. No independent test on different farms, seasons, cameras, or Ethiopian
     regions has been performed.

CURRENT DEPLOYMENT STATUS:
  The Holeta-trained model is an EXPERIMENTAL, LOCATION-SPECIFIC candidate.
  It is NOT a production-validated model. Deployment claims must wait for:
    - Leakage-resistant cross-validation
    - Independent field data from other farms/locations/seasons
    - Class-mapping and preprocessing verification

IMPLICATIONS FOR AGRIMIND-ETHIOPIA:
  These results support prioritizing Ethiopian field-image collection and
  expert-verified labels over relying exclusively on public datasets.
  They do NOT establish that:
    - 407 images are sufficient for broad deployment
    - A specific number of additional images (e.g., 2,000) will guarantee
      production-grade performance
    - The same results will transfer to other Ethiopian regions

  A larger dataset may help, but generalization depends on data quality,
  disease coverage, class balance, and geographic and seasonal diversity.

NEXT VALIDATION MILESTONES:
  1. Verify class mapping and preprocessing across all scripts and checkpoints
  2. Establish split integrity (leakage-free grouped CV)
  3. Preserve the current checkpoint as an experimental artifact
  4. Obtain independent expert-verified wheat images from another collection
  5. Evaluate the frozen model on that independent dataset

  Until those checks are complete, report 95.08% as preliminary internal
  evidence, not as a validated deployment result.
