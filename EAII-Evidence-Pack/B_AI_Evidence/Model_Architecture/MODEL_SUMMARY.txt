AGRIMIND AI MODEL — TECHNICAL SUMMARY
======================================

Architecture: MobileNetV2 (PyTorch)
Method: Transfer Learning (ImageNet pretrained weights)
Input Size: 128 × 128 × 3 (RGB)

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
=================
- Trained on PlantVillage-derived public imagery, not Ethiopian field photos
- No expert-verified or lab-confirmed Ethiopian images
- Not validated on real Ethiopian farm conditions
- v2's 15-class scope excludes 4 rare diseases (coffee/maize/wheat)
- v2 requires UI-level crop selection before safe deployment
  (closed-set classifier limitation — coffee/maize/wheat inputs would be
  misclassified as tomato/pepper/potato diseases)
- Evaluation is a partial leakage-control sensitivity analysis,
  not a full leakage-free audit (one train/val byte-identical duplicate
  remains, effect not independently quantified)
