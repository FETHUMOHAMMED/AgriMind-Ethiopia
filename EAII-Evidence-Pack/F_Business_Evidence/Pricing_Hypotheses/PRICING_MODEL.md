# AGRIMIND PRICING HYPOTHESES

**Status:** To be validated through pilots
**Date:** [Current Date]

## Hypothesis 1: Institutional Pilots
- Price: $5,000-$20,000 per pilot
- Duration: 3 months
- Covers: Setup, training, dashboard, field support
- Target: 3 pilots in Year 1

## Hypothesis 2: Cooperative Subscriptions
- Price: $500-$5,000/year
- Depends on cooperative size
- Includes: Dashboard, analytics, member management
- Target: 2 cooperatives Year 1

## Hypothesis 3: API Access
- Price: $5,000-$20,000/year
- Or $0.01-$0.10 per diagnosis call
- Target: 1 partner Year 1

## Hypothesis 4: Farmer Premium
- Price: Free for basic, $2/month premium
- NOT a Year 1 priority
- Test only after institutional model validated

## Validation Plan
1. Test pilot pricing with 3 cooperatives
2. Survey willingness to pay in farmer research
3. Compare with Lersha/FarmerChat pricing
4. Adjust based on pilot feedback

## Key Assumption
Farmers do not pay. Institutions pay. This must be validated.
