# AgriMind Customer Discovery Plan

**Week:** [Insert dates]
**Target:** 5–8 DAs + 2–3 institutional buyers
**Goal:** Validate the problem, user, buyer, and workflow — before building more AI

## Part 1 — Development Agent Interviews (5–8 DAs)

**Duration:** 45–60 min each
**Format:** Semi-structured, in person or phone
**Recruitment:** Via cooperative contacts, ARC extension officers, WhatsApp groups

### Background (5 min)
1. How long have you worked as a DA?
2. Which crops do farmers in your area grow most?
3. How many crop-problem cases do you handle per week, on average?

### Real Case (15 min — CRITICAL)
*Ask about a SPECIFIC recent case, not a hypothetical.*

4. Tell me about the most recent crop problem you helped a farmer with.
   - What was the crop and symptom?
   - How did you identify the problem?
   - What was the outcome?
5. Walk me through what you did step by step.
6. What information would have helped you identify it faster?

### Current Tools (10 min)
7. What resources do you use today to identify crop problems?
   (Manuals? WhatsApp? Senior agronomists? Nothing?)
8. When you can't identify something, what do you do?
9. How long does it take to get advice you trust?
10. What's the most frustrating part of your current process?

### Treatment Recommendations (10 min)
11. When you give farmers treatment advice, where does that advice come from?
12. How often does the first treatment work? What happens when it doesn't?
13. Have you ever seen a farmer waste money on a treatment that didn't work?
    (If yes, ask for a specific example.)

### AI-Assisted Diagnosis (10 min)
*Do NOT ask "Would you use an AI app?" Everyone says yes.*

14. Have you used any digital tool for crop diagnosis before?
    (If yes: what was it, did it work, do you still use it?)
15. If you had a tool that suggested a diagnosis from a photo, what would
    make you trust it?
16. What would make you distrust it?
17. What would you do if the tool said "I don't know" or "low confidence"?

### Cooperative Context (5 min)
18. How does your cooperative support your work today?
19. If your cooperative offered you a new tool, who would decide whether
    to use it?
20. Have you ever seen the cooperative pay for a service like this before?

### Closing
21. Is there anything I should have asked but didn't?
22. Would you be willing to participate in a 4-week pilot with a prototype?

## Part 2 — Buyer Interviews (2–3 Cooperative Managers / Union Reps)

**Duration:** 45–60 min each
**Format:** In person or Zoom/phone with video

### Background (10 min)
1. How many member farmers does your cooperative/union serve?
2. Which crops do your members grow most?
3. How do you currently support DAs in the field?

### Problem Awareness (10 min)
4. What are the biggest challenges your members face with crop diseases?
5. How much does a typical crop loss cost a member farmer?
6. What do you do when a DA can't identify a disease?

### Purchasing Authority (15 min)
7. Who decides whether to purchase advisory tools or services?
8. What budget line would this come from?
9. What's the annual procurement cycle for agricultural services?
10. What evidence would you need before approving a pilot?
11. What data protection or procurement requirements apply?

### Commercial Interest (10 min)
12. Have you ever paid for an advisory or extension service before?
13. If a 4-week pilot demonstrated value, would you consider a paid
    continuation?
14. What would the pilot success criteria need to be?

### Closing
15. Who else in your network should I speak with?

## Week-End Deliverable: Customer Discovery Report

Produce `CUSTOMER_DISCOVERY_REPORT_[date].md` with:

### 1. Summary (1 paragraph)
What did we learn this week?

### 2. Top 3 Validated Problems
For each:
- Problem statement (one sentence)
- Frequency cited across interviews (X out of Y interviews)
- Current workaround
- Cost of problem (time, money, yield loss if quantifiable)

### 3. Intended User
- Who is the user? (DA / field officer / agronomist?)
- What are they actually trying to accomplish?

### 4. Likely Buyer
- Which organization type? (cooperative / union / NGO / government?)
- Who has purchasing authority?
- What budget exists?
- What procurement process applies?

### 5. Evidence FOR Pilot
- Quote evidence: specific things DAs and buyers said
- Concrete commitments (named pilot owners, budget discussions)

### 6. Evidence AGAINST Pilot
- Objections raised
- Reasons for skepticism
- Gaps in the demand case

### 7. Decision
- **GO** — Pilot with named partner, budget discussion initiated
- **REVISE** — Problem validated, but buyer/user mismatch or scope needs adjustment
- **STOP** — No compelling problem found in this segment
