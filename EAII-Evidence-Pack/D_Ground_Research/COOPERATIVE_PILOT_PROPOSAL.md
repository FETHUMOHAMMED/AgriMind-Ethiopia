# AgriMind Cooperative Pilot — Proposal

**Proposer:** AgriMind-Ethiopia
**Duration:** 4 weeks
**Budget:** $0–500 (covered by AgriMind; cooperative contributes advisor time)
**Status:** Pre-pilot exploration — not a confirmed partnership

## What we are testing

We are testing whether a photo-based crop-disease assistant helps Development
Agents (DAs) provide faster, more consistent advice to farmers — **and where it
should NOT be used.**

This is a workflow pilot, not a validated diagnostic rollout.

## What we provide

1. **Free access** to the AgriMind mobile app for 5–10 DAs for 4 weeks
2. **Two-mode operation:**
   - **AI-assisted diagnosis** for tomato, pepper, and potato
   - **Workflow-only mode** for coffee, maize, wheat, and teff (DA photographs
     and escalates to expert; no AI diagnosis)
3. **Training session** (60 min) for participating DAs
4. **Weekly check-in** with the pilot coordinator (AgriMind side)
5. **Pilot report** at the end with all metrics documented

## What we ask from the cooperative

1. **Named pilot owner** (single point of contact)
2. **5–10 volunteer DAs** for the 4-week period
3. **Permission for DAs to spend 15–20 min/week extra** on the pilot
4. **Feedback channel** for DAs to report issues or suggestions

## Success metrics (all must be reported, none hidden)

### Technical
- % of cases falling within AI-supported classes (target: ≥ 80%)
- % of AI diagnoses agreed by agronomist on 20 sampled cases (target: ≥ 85%)

### Safety
- Unsafe or unsupported treatment recommendations (target: **0**)
- Cases where DA overrode AI diagnosis (target: tracked, not necessarily zero)

### Workflow
- Time per case: AI-assisted vs. baseline (target: ≥ 30% reduction)
- DA satisfaction score (target: ≥ 4/5)

### Commercial
- Cooperative's willingness to discuss a paid continuation (yes/no)
- Named budget source identified (yes/no)

## What this pilot does NOT prove

- Diagnostic accuracy on Ethiopian farm images (models trained on PlantVillage)
- Safety of treatment recommendations at scale (agronomist review required)
- Economic impact on farmer yields or input costs (requires longer study)
- Coverage of coffee, maize, wheat, or teff (outside model scope)

## What the cooperative receives at the end

- A written report with all metrics, including negative findings
- A proposal for a Phase 2 paid pilot if metrics support it
- No obligation to continue
- All data and findings from the pilot

## Timeline

## Risk to the cooperative

**Low.** Pilot is free, DAs can opt out at any time, no farmer-facing claims
will be made without explicit consent, and the two-mode design means coffee/
maize/wheat/teff farmers are not exposed to AI diagnosis.
