# Development Agent (DA) Interview Guide

**For:** Semi-structured interviews with 5–8 Agricultural Development Agents
**Duration:** 45–60 minutes
**Goal:** Understand current crop-problem workflows, unmet needs, and attitudes
toward AI-assisted diagnosis

## Before the interview

- Confirm DA has worked in the field for ≥ 6 months
- Confirm the DA handles at least 10 crop-problem cases per week
- Get consent to take notes (not record unless explicit permission)
- Note: crop mix in their service area, region, cooperative affiliation

## Section 1 — Background (5 min)

1. How long have you been a DA?
2. Which crops do farmers in your area grow most?
3. On average, how many crop-problem cases do you handle per week?

## Section 2 — Recent real case (15 min) — CRITICAL SECTION

> Ask about a SPECIFIC, RECENT case, not a hypothetical.

4. Tell me about the most recent crop problem you helped a farmer with.
   - What was the crop and symptom?
   - How did you identify the problem?
   - What was the outcome?
5. Walk me through what you did, step by step, from the first photo to the
   final decision.
6. What information would have helped you identify it faster?

## Section 3 — Current tools and workarounds (10 min)

7. What resources do you use today to identify crop problems?
   - (Extension manuals? WhatsApp groups? Senior agronomists? Nothing?)
8. When you don't know the answer, what do you do?
9. How long does it typically take to get advice you trust?
10. What's the most frustrating part of your current process?

## Section 4 — Treatment recommendations (10 min)

11. When you give farmers treatment advice, where does that advice come from?
12. How often does the first treatment work? What happens when it doesn't?
13. Have you ever seen a farmer waste money on a treatment that didn't work?
    (If yes, ask for a specific example.)

## Section 5 — AI-assisted diagnosis (10 min) — AVOID LEADING

> Do NOT ask "Would you use an AI app?" (everyone says yes).

14. Have you used any digital tool for crop diagnosis before?
    - If yes: what was it, did it work, do you still use it?
    - If no: why not?
15. If you had a tool that suggested a diagnosis from a photo, what would make
    you trust it?
16. What would make you distrust it?
17. What would you do if the tool said "I don't know" or "low confidence"?

## Section 6 — Cooperative relationship (5 min)

18. How does your cooperative support your work today?
19. If your cooperative offered you a new tool, who would decide whether to use it?
20. Have you ever seen the cooperative pay for a service like this before?

## Closing

21. Is there anything I should have asked but didn't?
22. Would you be willing to participate in a 4-week pilot with a prototype tool?

## Analysis after each interview

- Log in `DA_INTERVIEW_LOG.csv`:
  - Interview ID, date, region, crops served
  - Top 3 problems mentioned (verbatim quotes)
  - Current tool(s) used
  - Attitude toward AI (positive/neutral/skeptical)
  - Willingness to pilot (yes/maybe/no)
  - Key quote

## Red flags to watch for

- DA says "yes" to AI without hesitation → likely social-desirability bias
- DA can't name a specific recent case → they don't actually handle cases
- DA already uses a well-functioning digital tool → adoption barrier
- DA says "farmers would never trust this" → adoption barrier at the farmer level
